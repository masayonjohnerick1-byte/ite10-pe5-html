35 minutes · easy · serves 2

Crispy chickpea bowls

Roasted chickpeas bring crunch and warmth to a generous bowl of greens and vegetables. A lemony tahini dressing ties it all together.

Ingredients

  • 1 (400 g) can chickpeas, drained, rinsed, and patted dry
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • ½ teaspoon ground cumin
  • ½ teaspoon paprika
  • ¼ teaspoon salt
  • 2 cups cooked rice or quinoa
  • 2 cups chopped greens
  • 1 small cucumber, sliced
  • 1 small carrot, grated
  • 2 tablespoons tahini
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice and 1–2 tablespoons water

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 220°C (425°F). Line a rimmed baking tray with baking paper.
  2. Dry the chickpeas well with a clean towel. Toss them with olive oil, cumin, paprika, and salt.
  3. Spread chickpeas in one layer and roast for 25–30 minutes, shaking the tray once, until browned and crisp at the edges.
  4. Whisk tahini and lemon juice in a small bowl. Add water a teaspoon at a time until the dressing is pourable.
  5. Divide warm rice, greens, cucumber, and carrot between two bowls. Top with the roasted chickpeas.
  6. Drizzle with tahini dressing and serve while the chickpeas are still crisp.

Easy swaps

Use couscous instead of rice, add chopped tomatoes, or replace the tahini dressing with plain yogurt and lemon. Let the chickpeas cool uncovered to keep their edges crisp.

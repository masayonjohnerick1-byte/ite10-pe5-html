40 minutes · easy · serves 3–4

Skillet shakshuka

A one-pan meal of soft eggs nestled into a savory tomato and pepper sauce. Serve with bread for scooping.

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 medium onion, diced
  • 1 red bell pepper, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • ½ teaspoon sweet paprika
  • 1 (400 g) can crushed tomatoes
  • ¼ teaspoon salt and a few grinds of black pepper
  • 4 large eggs
  • 2 tablespoons chopped parsley or cilantro
  • Crusty bread, to serve

Method

  1. Warm the olive oil in a lidded skillet over medium heat. Add onion and bell pepper; cook for 6–8 minutes until softened.
  2. Stir in garlic, cumin, and paprika. Cook for 30 seconds, stirring so the garlic does not burn.
  3. Add crushed tomatoes, salt, and pepper. Bring to a gentle simmer and cook uncovered for 10–12 minutes, until slightly thickened.
  4. Use the back of a spoon to make four small wells in the sauce. Crack one egg into each well.
  5. Cover the skillet and cook for 5–8 minutes, checking often, until the whites are set and the yolks are cooked to your preference.
  6. Scatter parsley or cilantro over the top. Bring the skillet to the table and serve with bread.

Food-safety note

Eggs may be undercooked when the yolks are runny. For people at higher risk from foodborne illness, cook until both whites and yolks are firm.
